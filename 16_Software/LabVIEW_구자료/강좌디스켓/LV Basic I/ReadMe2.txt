This disk accompanies the LabVIEW Basics I Course manual.  

The file named Bas1Soln.exe is a self-extracting archive that contains the solutions to all the course exercises.  When you double-click on this file, the LV Bas1 Soln directory will be installed in the C:\Solutions directory.  

You will need LabVIEW 6.0 or later to open and use these VIs.
